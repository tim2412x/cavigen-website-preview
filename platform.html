MicroSphere

A defined starting point for spheroids and organoids.

  • Standardised 3D aggregation
  • Imaging and standard 3D assays
  • Inserts for 12-well plates or films
Explore a trial ↗
More about MicroSphere

Defined microcavities, typically 300–800 µm in diameter, support 3D aggregation and microscopy. Cavity geometry and protocol should be matched to your cell model.

Full technical information ↗

PoroSphere

Porous microcavities for exchange and interaction.

  • Dynamic culture and co-culture
  • Barrier and permeability studies
  • Inserts or films for integration
Discuss your model ↗
More about PoroSphere

Porous cavity walls allow exchange with the surrounding environment. Films can be integrated into microfluidic and Organ-on-Chip systems. Porosity and layout can be discussed for your application.

Full technical information ↗

SensoSphere

Bring local oxygen into the picture.

  • Integrated optical oxygen sensing
  • Oxygen dynamics in 3D models
  • Sensor inserts or sensor films
Discuss a demonstration ↗
More about SensoSphere

An oxygen-sensitive layer enables local readouts in the microenvironment of 3D cultures. Use suitable fluorescence microscopy or dedicated readout equipment. Optical configuration and calibration must match the experiment.

A few useful answers.

Can I use my existing microscope?

MicroSphere is designed for microscopy and standard 3D assays. SensoSphere needs suitable fluorescence optics or a dedicated readout device. Send us your instrument configuration so we can discuss the appropriate setup.

Which cell model should I start with?

The platform supports a range of spheroid, organoid and co-culture approaches. The cell type, seeding protocol and experimental endpoint determine the appropriate variant. Start by telling us about your model.

How do I get a trial or quotation?

Use “Explore a trial” on the relevant product. Your email app opens a draft to CAVIGEN. Availability, configuration, pricing and support can then be agreed directly with the team.
